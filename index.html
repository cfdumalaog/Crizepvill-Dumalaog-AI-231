What is AI?

🤖

Definition

Artificial Intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence by machines, enabling systems to learn, reason, and make decisions.

🧠

Machine Learning

A subset of AI where systems improve through experience, learning patterns from data without explicit programming.

🔮

Deep Learning

Uses multi-layered neural networks to model complex patterns, powering breakthroughs in vision, speech, and language.

Branches of AI

💬

Natural Language Processing

Enables machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language — powering chatbots, translation, and voice assistants.

👁️

Computer Vision

Allows systems to interpret and analyze visual information from the world, used in medical imaging, autonomous vehicles, and security.

🚗

Robotics

Combines AI with mechanical systems to create autonomous robots capable of performing complex physical tasks.

📊

Predictive Analytics

Uses historical data and statistical models to forecast future outcomes, aiding decision-making across industries.

AI Milestones

1950

Turing Test

Alan Turing proposes the "Imitation Game," laying the philosophical groundwork for AI.

1956

Term Coined

"Artificial Intelligence" is introduced at the Dartmouth Conference.

1997

Deep Blue

IBM's Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov.

2012

Deep Learning Breakthrough

AlexNet wins ImageNet, igniting the modern deep learning revolution.

2016

AlphaGo

DeepMind's AlphaGo defeats Go champion Lee Sedol, a milestone in complex reasoning.

2022

Generative AI

Large language models like GPT-3 and DALL-E bring AI-generated content to the mainstream.
